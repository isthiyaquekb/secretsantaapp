How it works

Three easy steps to complete your exchange draw in minutes.

1

Add names

Type participants directly or import your team list from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. Duplicate detection and column preview included.

2

Draw

Generate a fair, randomized single-cycle draw with one click. Guaranteed that nobody draws themselves.

3

Reveal in private

Pass the device around. Each person holds to reveal their assigned recipient, then releases to hide it before the next turn.
