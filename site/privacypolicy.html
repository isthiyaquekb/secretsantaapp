Privacy Policy ★

Secret Santa · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Secret Santa (“the app”) helps a group run a gift exchange. It is developed by Isthiyaque Kambathagothi. Questions about this policy can be sent to developer.isthiyaque@gmail.com.

What the app stores

To work, the app keeps the following on your device:

  • the names you type or import,
  • the draw pairings and who has revealed their pick,
  • the exchange date,
  • the HR PIN you set.

On the mobile apps this data is kept in the device’s secure storage. In the web version it is kept in your browser’s storage for this site, on your device only.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Names and pairings are not sent to any server.

Files you import

If you import an Excel or CSV file, the app reads it on your device to pick out the names. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Website hosting

When you open the web version, the hosting provider may keep standard technical logs of the request, such as IP address, browser type and time. We do not use these logs to identify individual users.

Deleting your data

Use “Reset draw” in the HR view to clear the pairings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, removes everything the app has stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
