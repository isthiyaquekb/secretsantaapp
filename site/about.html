About Secret Santa ★

The simple, private way to run holiday gift exchanges.

The Holiday Tradition

Secret Santa (also known as Kris Kringle or X-mas Friend) is a cherished holiday tradition where a group anonymously exchanges gifts. Each member randomly draws the name of one recipient to buy a present for, with the identity of the gift-giver kept a secret until the unwrapping party.

How the App Works

Traditionally, organizing an exchange meant cutting up pieces of paper, drawing them from a hat, and hoping no one pulled their own name. This app modernizes that process while keeping the human connection intact:

  • Easy Roster Setup: Enter names one by one or import an existing Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file in seconds.
  • Fair Randomization: The algorithm creates a continuous single cycle so everyone gives one gift and receives one gift, and no one ever draws themselves.
  • Pass-and-Reveal: Designed for an in-person pass-around. Participants hold down to view their assigned recipient privately and let go to conceal it.
  • Master PIN Protection: The organizer sets an HR PIN to discreetly verify pairings later if someone forgets their recipient.

Who It Is For

Secret Santa is built for any group celebrating together:

  • Workplace teams & offices seeking a hassle-free exchange without sending company emails to external servers.
  • Friends & families hosting annual holiday get-togethers.
  • Classrooms & student groups organizing end-of-term celebrations.
  • Clubs & community groups organizing festive events.

The Privacy-First Promise

We believe holiday traditions shouldn't require creating accounts, giving up email addresses, or having personal data tracked. All data stays on your device only. Names, pairings, and dates are saved in your local device storage and are never uploaded to any cloud server.
